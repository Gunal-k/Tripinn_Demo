FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need
to know before joining.

Around 75% of our travelers join completely solo! Our groups consist of young working professionals, solo backpackers, creative minds, and nature enthusiasts between 20 to 38 years old. We create a welcoming, respectful environment where you immediately feel at home.

Safety is our highest priority. Over half of our community are solo women. We maintain strict group conduct standards, provide separate female rooms/sharing arrangements, stay only in verified 3-star gated properties, and have experienced team leads present 24/7.

We provide convenient pickup and drop points along Chennai’s main corridor: Koyambedu (CMBT), Guindy (Kathipara Junction), and Tambaram (GST Road). Exact departure timings (usually Friday 8:30 PM–10:00 PM) and live location tracking are shared in the trip WhatsApp group.

The price covers sanitized round-trip AC tempo traveller transportation from Chennai, toll/parking charges, 3-star hotel/resort stay (twin/triple sharing), daily breakfast and dinner, guided sightseeing, and evening bonfires with music. Personal snacks, lunch, and entry tickets not specified are paid directly.

You pay ₹3,000 via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) to confirm your seat slot. The remaining balance is payable on the trip start date. If your plans change, cancellations made 10 days or more before the departure date receive a 100% full refund immediately.
